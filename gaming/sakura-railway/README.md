# 春の途中 — Sakura Railway

Fullscreen Three.js voxel cinematic, deployed as a subpage of the Gaming Lab:

https://chenmzh.github.io/chenmzh.github.io-repository/gaming/sakura-railway/

- 42-second loop: approach, warning bell, gates down, two opposing commuter trains, gates up, four students crossing
- Double-track railway with independent track beds, rails, sleepers, catenary and two original train liveries
- Four slender Japanese high-school students wait in pairs, then cross with staggered timing and individual walk cycles
- A steep uphill residential street and a quick downhill street continue beyond the crossing
- Procedural houses, retaining walls, drainage, guardrails, mailboxes, layered green trees, cherry blossoms and petals
- Opt-in synthesized wind, birds, crossing bell and distance-aware train rumble; no external audio or film assets
- Desktop pointer parallax, mobile composition, reduced-motion support and LOW / MEDIUM / HIGH quality tiers
