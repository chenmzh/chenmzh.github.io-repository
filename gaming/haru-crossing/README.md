# Haru Crossing — 春の途中

Fullscreen Three.js voxel cinematic, deployed as an independent Gaming Lab subpage:

https://chenmzh.github.io/chenmzh.github.io-repository/gaming/haru-crossing/

- 42-second loop: approach / warning / wait / opposing trains / barrier lift / crossing / departure
- Double-track railway with two commuter trains moving in opposite directions
- Four slender Japanese high-school students cross between steep uphill and downhill residential streets
- Procedural houses, walls, trees, cherry blossoms, catenary, crossing equipment and neighborhood details
- Scroll-controlled timeline, responsive desktop/mobile compositions and opt-in synthesized ambience
- Vite + Three.js, fully procedural geometry with adaptive quality tiers
