The games keep their own purpose-built interfaces; their entry points now share the site system.

GAME 01 / PHYSICS Chroma Drop Tilt a three-dimensional liquid-metal chamber, activate every charge node and preserve enough mass to reach the drain. WebGL / Precision / Touch GAME 02 / STRATEGY Iron Dawn Harvest crystal fields, build a bright sci-fi base, command ground and air forces, and destroy the enemy headquarters. Canvas RTS / Land + Air / Bilingual GAME 03 / RELATIVITY GARGANTUA A fullscreen Schwarzschild black-hole raytracer: real null-geodesic integration, gravitational lensing, a photon ring and a turbulent accretion disk. WebGL2 / Raytracer / Real-time GAME 04 / CINEMA Sakura Railway A 150-second voxel film: a silver commuter train surfaces from the morning mist, crosses the cherry-blossom mid-ground and leaves through the right edge — scroll to drag the timeline. WebGL / Voxel / Scroll-driven GAME 05 / COLLECTION Cloud Patisserie Choose from a fixed blind-box case, read weight and sound clues, reveal 25 animated pixel characters, and earn the next box through packing or quick-fire quizzes. Pixel Art / Blind Box / Math + RL GAME 06 / CINEMA Haru Crossing A 42-second double-track railway film: four students wait through two opposing commuter trains, then cross toward steep uphill and downhill neighborhood streets.
